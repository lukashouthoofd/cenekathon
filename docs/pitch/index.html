01 — PROBLEM
Workforce decisions are made blind to the market.
HR knows who they have. The job market shows what's needed. Nobody connects the two.
01
Skills data lives in HR systems, with no outside benchmark.
02
Market demand is buried in millions of unstructured job postings.
03
Even when a gap is found, nobody owns the next step: train, buy, build or hire?
02 — THE QUESTION
“Which skills is the market hiring for that our people don't have yet?”
Today that's a consulting project. Json answers it in Slack, then does something about it.
03 — SOLUTION
Json finds the skill gap, then routes it to the right fix.
Spots the gap
Benchmarks your team's skills against 1.5M real vacancies and peer companies.
Picks the fix
Train, tool or hire, with the evidence behind the choice.
Hands it off in Slack
Sends the plan straight to L&D, your engineers or HR.
04 — THREE ACTIONS
One gap. Three ways to close it.
Train
→ L&D · TEAM LEADS
Proposes in-company lectures and training (opleidingen) for the people who need the skill.
Tool
→ SOFTWARE ENGINEERS
Proposes buying a SaaS tool, or asks your engineers in Slack to build an internal one.
Hire
→ HR
When the skill must come from outside, alerts HR in Slack and drafts the vacancy.
05 — HIRING FLOW
From skill gap to vacancy, with HR in control.
1Json spots a gap that training or tools can't close
2Json drafts a custom vacancy, based on real market postings for the role
3HR fine-tunes the draft
4HR publishes it, or recruits potential hires on LinkedIn
