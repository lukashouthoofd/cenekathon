A5 — LIKELY JUDGE QUESTIONS
Six questions we expect, with honest answers.
Is the vacancy data real?
Yes: 1.34M deduplicated finance vacancies, licensed for team use only. Internal bank data is synthetic and labelled as such.
Are the skill figures verified skills?
No. They are keyword counts on titles and descriptions, and the chat tells the model to say so.
Why not just ask an LLM for everything?
The numbers come from SQL. Claude reads about 20 summary numbers per company, which is cheaper and checkable.
How reliable are the company AI scores?
They are model inferences from public postings only. We never claim a company's real AI spend. Raw probabilities are kept for audit.
Is it privacy-safe?
No named individuals. Team-level aggregates only, and synthetic data in the demo.
What happens after the hackathon?
A pilot on one bank's real HR data. TechWolf models for title and skill mapping are planned, not built yet (slide 13b).
